Mithila Heritage & Artisanal Craftsmanship

The Sacred Journey — From Farm to Plate

From tranquil wetland lotus ponds in Bihar to the crunchy delight in your dining bowl. Here is how authentic, unadulterated Prishka Annam makhana is nurtured, harvested, and crafted step-by-step.

01 Pond Sowing

Freshwater Wetland Cultivation

Makhana (Euryale ferox) thrives naturally in the tranquil freshwater wetland ponds of North Bihar. Nourished by fertile alluvial silt and pure monsoon rains, the plants grow completely free of artificial chemicals or synthetic fertilizers.

🌿 Direct from Mithila Ponds
02 Dawn Harvest

Deep-Water Indigenous Diving

At dawn, master harvesters navigate the ponds in wooden canoes. Skilled divers submerge beneath floating spiny leaves to sweep the pond bed with traditional bamboo sieves (Ganja), gathering the prized black lotus seeds by hand.

🛶 Generational Diving Wisdom
03 Sun-Curing

Solar Washing & Bamboo Drying

The harvested seeds are thoroughly cleaned in fresh running water to remove silt, then spread evenly on woven bamboo mats. They bake under the warm Bihar sun for 48 to 72 hours, slowly tempering the shells to perfect popping density.

☀️ 100% Natural Solar Dried
04 Artisan Popping

Cast-Iron Roasting & Mallet Striking

The dried seeds are roasted in earthen and heavy cast-iron pans over open wood fires. While scorching hot, each seed is struck with a traditional wooden mallet (Thapi) — cracking the black shell with a pop to unleash snow-white puffs.

🔥 Hand-Struck with Wooden Thapi
05 Manual Sorting

Multi-Tier Handpick Grading (4+, 5+, 6+)

Every batch is hand-winnowed to eliminate broken shells, unpopped kernels, and residue. The puffs are meticulously sized into 4+ Classic Daily, 5+ Large Crunch, and 6+ Super Jumbo — ensuring zero burnt pieces or hard kernels.

✨ Zero Unpopped / Broken Seeds
06 Farm to Plate

Farm-Fresh Crunch to Your Table

Packed and nitrogen-flushed immediately in multi-layer zip-lock barrier pouches at origin. Zero chlorine bleaching, zero artificial shine, and zero additives — delivering wholesome, guilt-free crunch straight into your daily routine.

🍽️ Farm-Fresh & Direct to Home
